THE BEGINNING

Village roots, global outlook

Bilal hails from Chak No. 7 in Mandi Bahauddin, Punjab. His videos celebrate his “Pind” roots, local traditions, and pride in Pakistani village life.

His rise on social media grew from this contrast: a taste for luxury and international experiences, paired with an approachable village personality.

Before his online rise, Bilal spent a significant part of his life in Paris, France. His updated biography describes a family construction and building-renovation business operating there for around 30–40 years.
